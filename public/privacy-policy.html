Privacy Policy for APEX Workout Tracker

Last updated: November 17, 2026

APEX ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data within the APEX: Minimalist Workout Tracker mobile and web application.

1. The Core Privacy Principle: Zero Cloud Tracking

APEX is built on a Privacy-First Architecture. We believe your workout records, body measurements, weights lifted, and training schedules belong solely to you.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register with an email, phone number, or social login to use APEX.
  • Local Storage on Device: All workout history, custom routines, exercise notes, and personal records (PRs) are stored locally on your device storage.
  • No Telemetry or Ad Trackers: We do not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, or data brokers.

2. Data Collected & Permissions Used

APEX only utilizes standard Android permissions strictly required for core workout tracking features:

  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback when you mark a set as complete or when your rest timer countdown finishes.
  • Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK): Used optionally during an active workout session so your phone screen does not sleep while resting between heavy sets.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Used solely to load web fonts and optional CDN assets. No workout data is ever transmitted across the internet.

3. Data Backup & Export

You have 100% control over your training data. APEX includes an offline JSON export and import utility. You can export your complete database anytime to keep your own backups or transfer data to a new device.

4. Children's Privacy

APEX does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
